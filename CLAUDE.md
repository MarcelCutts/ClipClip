# Out of the red

Static Astro 7 + Svelte 5 site teaching DJs and crew to keep set recordings clean (Pioneer XDJ-RX2 → Howler recorder on MASTER 2; DriveRack PA2 and QSC GX7 amps on the PA; booth monitors on BOOTH). The header has three entrances: Playing at `/`, Crew at `/night/` and Learn at `/learn/`. The crew's other pages are one step from Crew: the rig reference (wiring and settings) at `/setup/`, file handling at `/recordings/` and the print kit at `/print/`. See README.md for pages, commands and deployment, and docs/DESIGN.md for the visual system.

## Commands

- `pnpm dev` (Astro 7 backgrounds itself when it detects an agent; stop it with `pnpm exec astro dev stop`)
- `pnpm verify` runs lint, check, unit tests, build and e2e. E2E needs `pnpm build` first and serves on port 4322.
- `pnpm clipcheck <files.wav>` checks recordings for clipping (`src/lib/clipcheck/`, documented in the README). Its tests are the calibration: keep them passing when you tune it.

## Conventions

- Facts about the gear come from the manufacturers' documents listed in `src/lib/sources.ts`. If Pioneer or Howler don't publish something, say so on the page rather than guessing.
- Simulation assumptions live only in `src/lib/model.ts` and `src/lib/xdj.ts`. Widgets import them; never hard-code a ceiling.
- Copy: a reference guide's register, not an assistant's; the rules are in docs/DESIGN.md's Voice section. In short: UK English, second person, short sentences, every line with one reader and one job. Repeat a required condition where an independently opened procedure needs it. Hardware names are as printed (TRIM, MASTER LEVEL, BOOTH MONITOR, the MASTER meters). Write "recording level", "orange" (not "yellow"), and "crunch" (not THD) on DJ-facing pages. Conditions come first ("If …, …"). No ", so" chains, idioms or negative contractions. Checklist lines end in a state you can see. Shared wording lives in `src/lib/rules.ts`, `myths.ts`, `glossary.ts`, `checklists.ts`, `fixes.ts`, `messages.ts`.
- Styles use the tokens in `src/styles/tokens.css`; interactives sit in `.panel` hardware boxes, except the checklists and the meter check's quiz cards (confidence is optional), which are printed QRH cards (`--paper` card, `--strip` title, `--action` responses). Black panels are only for real gear (meters, faders, scopes, the Howler). Anything shown as paper in both themes carries `data-paper`. Chart marks use `--sig`, `--sig-b` and `--dmg` only.
- The look is a quick reference handbook (docs/DESIGN.md): B612 text, know-by-heart boxes (`KnowByHeart.astro`), procedure cards (`Procedure.astro`), challenge/response lines with the response in `--action`, numbered sections from `src/lib/sections.ts`. Flat panels, two radii, no pills or side stripes; plain voice ("If …, …"), no slogans.
- The site follows the reader's light/dark setting. Page tokens (`--paper`, `--ink*`, `--action`, `--tint`) change; hardware tokens (`--hw*`, `--led-*`, `--screen*`) don't. Text on a panel uses hardware tokens, text on the page uses page tokens. Check both themes. Lit LED colours only ever mean a signal state.
- Islands must render a meaningful state on the server and never touch `window` or Web Audio during SSR. All sound goes through `src/lib/audio/engine.svelte.ts` (or `loopPlayer.ts`), never autoplays, and clean/clipped comparisons are loudness-matched.
- Keep `typescript` on 6.x until `@astrojs/check` and `svelte-check` support 7.

- Offline packages are built from the actual output (`scripts/offline.ts`, `src/lib/offline-worker.ts`). Never report readiness on registration alone or force a waiting update into an open event. Test under `/ClipClip/` as well as `/`.
- Keep C1 as one linear list in the order of the work (Table, Leads, Power, Levels, Record). It stands alone: no line sends the reader to another card to carry on. Its printed version uses two cards and a card of drawings; all consume `checklists.ts` and `figures.ts`.
- This is a guide for the field. Nothing on it needs a laptop except the next day's work on the files (`/recordings/`). Do not add tests or commissioning procedures: T2, T3, S3, S5 and S6 were removed on 28 September 2026, and their old links forward. Never cite a card, test or section that does not exist.
- C1's drawings live in `src/components/figures/` (`Fig.astro` holds their shared classes). A line names its drawings in `checklists.ts` (`figures`), and `/night/` hands them to the island as named slots, so they are in the served HTML. Draw shapes inline: styles do not reach inside an SVG `<use>`. A filled path must close; a line that stays open carries `open` (`figures.test.ts`). Draw diagrams, never photos.
- The gear carries no tape or marks, and none are planned. A line ends in a light, a meter or a position anyone can see (fully down, switched on). MASTER LEVEL is set at soundcheck by the MASTER meters (Pioneer p. 31) and the room by the amps' gain knobs with their CLIP lights dark. The tape tags are optional labels. No checklist or drill changes MASTER ATT: the recording comes down at MASTER LEVEL (F1) and the room comes back up at the amps.
- The rig as built: a RocknRoller R12 trolley is the table (top bolted to both handles, a booth monitor's bracket under each bolt). The rack rides on its bed with its knobs to the crowd: top amp to the tops, DriveRack in the middle, bottom amp to the subs. Booth monitors (the box pro Achat) are on BOOTH. Which amp channel is left or right does not matter. The generator changes with the event.
- Hum, earthing, RCDs and generators are out of scope. F10 (hum or buzz) was retired on 28 September 2026 and its number is not reused. Do not add a drill for anything that can only be found by hearing a recording at the event.
- The site gives no hearing or health advice. The hearing warning on Playing was removed on 28 September 2026, with the documents that served only it.
- A drill lives where its fault is found (`where` in `fixes.ts`): the booth's on `/night/`, and what is found by listening the day after (F9, F11) on `/recordings/`.
- Every page is built the same way (docs/DESIGN.md, Layout): `SiteHeader.astro` with the site's name at the top left, `PageHeader.astro` as the title block, then a `.page-body`. Use the three gaps in `tokens.css` (`--gap-text`, `--gap-block`, `--space-section`) and the classes in `global.css` (`.page-section`, `.page-group`, `.section-start`, `.link-row`, `.deflist`); a page does not set its own title size, title spacing or gaps. `tests/e2e/page-anatomy.spec.ts` holds every page to this. Links in a row are spaced apart, with no dots printed between them.
- Playing (`/`) opens with the reason, then the drawing of the meters (`MeterTargets.astro`), then the three rules. The drawing keeps the two meters apart: a ring round the channels' first orange and round the MASTER pair's top orange, each with its rule beside it in the rule's own words (`drawn` in `rules.ts`), and no numbered key. The share image and the booth card draw the same. Each instruction is said once, and nothing on the page is for the crew. The meters' scale prints as the panel does, with no plus signs (`scaleLabel`). The DJ briefing links to the top of Playing (`GUIDE_PATH`); the booth card's QR code lands on Set TRIM (`CARD_PATH`).
- The recording cannot be played back in the booth. No list asks for a test recording on site; listening happens the next day (`/recordings/`).
